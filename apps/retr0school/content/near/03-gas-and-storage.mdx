# Gas and Storage

NEAR charges for two different things.

## Gas: computation

Every transaction burns **gas**, measured in **Tgas** (10^12 gas). Gas has a price in NEAR set by the network, and it is low and stable. A simple contract call is a few Tgas, and one transaction can attach up to **300 Tgas**.

- Unused gas is refunded.
- 30% of the gas burned by a contract call is paid to that **contract's account**, so builders earn from usage.

## Storage: bytes on chain

State is not paid per transaction. Instead the account that **holds** the data must keep enough NEAR locked: about **1 NEAR per 100 KB** (10^19 yocto per byte). This is called **storage staking**. Delete the data and the NEAR unlocks again.

That is why many contracts ask you to attach a small **deposit**. The Retr0 Academy diploma contract does this: you attach 0.02 NEAR when you mint, the contract measures how many bytes your diploma used, keeps exactly that, and **refunds the rest** in the same call.

```rust
// from contracts/diploma/src/lib.rs
let token = self.tokens.internal_mint_with_refund(
    token_id, account_id.clone(), Some(metadata), Some(account_id),
);
```

<Tip>

Rule of thumb: view calls are free, change calls cost gas, and anything that adds state needs a storage deposit.

</Tip>

## Quick numbers

| Thing | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| Transfer NEAR | about 0.45 Tgas |
| Mint one diploma | under 20 Tgas, plus a storage deposit of about 0.01 NEAR |
| 100 KB of contract state | 1 NEAR locked |
