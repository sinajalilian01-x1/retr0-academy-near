# Tokens and NFTs

Two kinds of on-chain assets show up in almost every Web3 app.

**Fungible tokens** are interchangeable. One USDC is the same as any other USDC. On Ethereum the common interface is ERC-20 (`transfer`, `approve`, `transferFrom`, `balanceOf`). On NEAR the equivalent standard is **NEP-141**, with `ft_transfer`, `ft_balance_of`, `ft_transfer_call`, and a storage deposit so the contract can pay for your balance row.

**Non-fungible tokens** are unique. A diploma, a profile picture, or a ticket is not interchangeable with another. Ethereum uses ERC-721. NEAR uses **NEP-171**, with `nft_token`, `nft_transfer`, and metadata in NEP-177. The Retr0 Academy diploma is a *soulbound* NEP-171 NFT: the contract refuses `nft_transfer`, so the credential stays with the account that earned it.

Amounts on NEAR are usually **strings of yocto-units** (1 NEAR = 10^24 yoctoNEAR). Wallets need strings so 24-decimal values do not get rounded in JavaScript.

You will implement NEP-141 and NEP-171 in the Tokens & NFTs on NEAR course. For now, remember the split: fungible vs unique, standard method names, and why a credential should not be tradable.
